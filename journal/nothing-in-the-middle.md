Every station on AuralFM belongs to somebody else. A community station in the Andes, a gospel hour in Lagos, a techno stream in Tbilisi, a college station in Ohio. None of them signed a deal with us. None of them are ranked.

That is the whole idea. Most of what we listen to now comes through a middle: a label deciding what gets pushed, a network deciding what gets played, and a feed deciding what you are allowed to find. The middle is where taste gets flattened and where your attention gets sold.

AuralFM tries to have as little middle as possible.

## Where things come from

- **The stations** are our own catalogue: each one confirmed by the community-run radio-browser database or by the station itself — its own stream and website answering — and placed in its town from GeoNames, an open atlas of the world's places.
- **The Earth** is NASA's, given away to everyone, with live cloud cover and the line between day and night where it truly falls.
- **The chat** rides open, decentralized relays that no single company owns. There is no account to make, and no profile that follows you from room to room.

When you press play, your device goes straight to the station. We are not relaying the audio, re-streaming it or putting an ad in front of it.

## What "no algorithm" means here

It does not mean nothing is sorted. You can sort by what is near, by hidden gems, by who is awake right now, and by vibes that people named. What it means is that nothing learns you. The globe does not get narrower the more you use it. A station in Kampala gets the same dot as one in London, and it keeps it.

Radio never went quiet. We just stopped being shown it. The middle is the thing that stopped showing it to us — so we left it out.
