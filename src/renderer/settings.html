Relays

AuralFM picks relays near the place you listen to. It uses a public directory and a few well-known relays. Add your own relay and AuralFM tries it first. AuralFM asks a relay only for messages in the cell you listen to or look at. No relay learns where you are.

This setting is off by default, because it is the only thing here that sends anything. When you turn it on, AuralFM announces the station you play: its name, and its location rounded to about 20 km. AuralFM never sends your location. Other people who run AuralFM then see that a listener is there. There is no account: AuralFM makes a key when it opens, and throws that key away when it closes. AuralFM marks the messages with a kind that asks relays not to keep them. Most relays honour that request, but nobody can guarantee it, so nothing in these messages is worth keeping.
