Relays

Relays are the servers that carry the messages. AuralFM picks ones near the place you are listening to. Add your own and it tries those first. No relay is ever told where you are.

Privacy

Nickname
Hide message text until opened
Your key here —

A nickname is not an account — nothing verifies it, and it lasts as long as the key it travels with. Your key is different in every place you listen to, and none of them are saved.

Keep one identity across sessions
Your lasting fingerprint —

Off, every key is thrown away when you quit, and nobody can favorite or verify you — there is nothing lasting to point at. On, one key is saved on this Mac and used for private messages only; presence on the globe stays anonymous either way. The cost is real: anyone watching relays can tell that the identity written to on Tuesday was written to again on Friday. Switching this off destroys the key, and anyone holding your fingerprint will find nothing answers to it.

Hide messages with words on the list
Only show people you keep
Word list

Nobody moderates this network — there is no server to run a moderator and no account to suspend, which is true of every app built this way. The word filter catches careless abuse, not determined abuse: a list cannot beat somebody who spaces out their letters. "Only people you keep" is the tool that actually holds, because being a stranger cannot be worked around. Reporting a message mutes the sender straight away and opens an email you choose whether to send.

Muted
People you keep

A tick means you compared fingerprints with this person yourself and they matched. Only you can set that, and only you should — read the sixteen characters aloud on a call or in person. A fingerprint somebody typed into the chat proves nothing.

Erase everything now

Favourites, listening history, the mute list and every key this session used. Appearance settings stay. This cannot be undone.
