Put AuralFM on your iPhone

Open listen.auralfm.xyz in Safari, then:

  1. Tap Share. It is in Safari’s ••• menu.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen. Scroll down to find it.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App.
  4. Tap Add.

AuralFM then opens from your Home Screen, full screen, like any other app. See the steps in the app.
