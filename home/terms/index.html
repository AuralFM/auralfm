AuralFM — Terms of Service

What AuralFM is

AuralFM is an application — a Mac app, and a web version you can open in a browser or add to a phone's home screen — that helps you find and play publicly available internet radio streams, and shows public information about the world alongside them.

AuralFM does not host, own, operate, or control any radio station, and does not host, store, or transmit any audio. When you press play, your computer connects directly to that station's own server. AuralFM is a directory and a player, in the same sense that a web browser is not the websites it visits.

The content is not ours

Station listings are AuralFM's own catalogue, confirmed from the public-domain radio-browser.info directory and from what stations publish about themselves: their streams, websites and social accounts. Town and region names come from GeoNames (geonames.org, CC BY 4.0). Broadcasts belong to the stations, and so do their names and logos, which AuralFM shows only to say which station is which. Satellite imagery, weather, headlines, market prices and orbital data come from the third parties named in the app's About panel and in the Privacy Policy.

We make no claim to any of it, and we cannot grant you rights to it. Your use of any stream is subject to that station's own terms and to the laws where you are.

Some stations do not play everywhere. In the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area, music stations from other countries are listed but do not play until the licensing there is settled; your own country's stations, and talk, news and sport from anywhere, do. Where you are is worked out from your device's time zone, on the device (see the Privacy Policy).

The chat

AuralFM has an optional chat tied to the area you are listening to. It is off until you turn it on, there is no account, and nothing you send is stored by us — we run no server. Messages travel over public relays operated by other people, and once sent, a message is out of our hands and cannot be recalled or deleted by us.

Because we operate no server, we cannot delete what other people send. You can mute anyone and hide messages by word, and you can report what you see (see the Code of Conduct below). Treat anything you read there as coming from a stranger. Do not send anything you would mind a stranger keeping.

Acceptable use

Do not use AuralFM to infringe copyright, to redistribute or rebroadcast streams you have no right to, to harass anyone through the chat, or to break the law where you are. The Code of Conduct says in plain words what is not welcome wherever people speak on AuralFM, and what happens when it turns up.

Child safety

Nothing that sexualises a child is tolerated anywhere on AuralFM. When we learn of it, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperate with law enforcement, and a station carrying it is removed from the catalogue. If you find it, do not copy or forward it: report it at report.cybertip.org, or to the police where you are, and then tell us.

Stations: corrections and removal

If you run a station, you can ask us to correct its listing (name, place, website, genre) or to take it off AuralFM altogether. Email hello@auralfm.xyz from an address at the station's own website, or tell us how else to confirm it is yours. We act on every such request. The page for stations also says how to claim your station's page with a file on your own website.
