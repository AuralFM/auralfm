## What AuralFM is

AuralFM is an application — a Mac app, and a web version you can open in a
browser or add to a phone's home screen — that helps you find and play publicly available
internet radio streams, and shows public information about the world alongside
them.

**AuralFM does not host, own, operate, or control any radio station**, and does
not host, store, or transmit any audio. When you press play, your computer
connects directly to that station's own server. AuralFM is a directory and a
player, in the same sense that a web browser is not the websites it visits.

## The content is not ours

Station listings are AuralFM's own catalogue, confirmed from the public-domain
radio-browser.info directory and from what stations publish about themselves:

4.0). Broadcasts belong to the stations, and so do their names and
logos, which AuralFM shows only to say which station is which. Satellite imagery, weather, headlines, market
prices and orbital data come from the third parties named in the app's About
panel and in the Privacy Policy.

We make no claim to any of it, and we cannot grant you rights to it. Your use of
any stream is subject to that station's own terms and to the laws where you are.

**Some stations do not play everywhere.** In the United Kingdom and the European
Economic Area, music stations from other countries are listed but do not play
until the licensing there is settled; your own country's stations, and talk,
news and sport from anywhere, do. Where you are is worked out from your device's
time zone, on the device (see the Privacy Policy).

## The chat

AuralFM has an optional chat tied to the area you are listening to. It is off
until you turn it on, there is no account, and nothing you send is stored by us —
we run no server. Messages travel over public relays operated by other people,
and **once sent, a message is out of our hands and cannot be recalled or
deleted by us.**

Because we operate no server, we cannot delete what other people send. You can
mute anyone and hide messages by word, and you can report what you see (see the
Code of Conduct below). Treat anything you read there as coming from a stranger.
Do not send anything you would mind a stranger keeping.

## Your show

AuralFM has a way to go live from inside the app ("Your show"). It is in beta,
in test builds of the Mac app. A show goes from the host's device straight to
the people listening, a small number at a time. These terms apply to it.

- **You must be 18 or over to go live or to announce a show.** Listening stays
  open to everyone 13 and over.
- **Play only what you have the right to play:** your own voice and music, and
  music you are licensed to broadcast. Playing other people's recordings to the
  public needs licences (in the US from SoundExchange and the songwriters' bodies,
  in the UK from PPL and PRS); when the sound goes from your device to listeners,
  **you are the broadcaster** and those licences are yours to hold.
- The [Code of Conduct](https://docs.auralfm.xyz/conduct/) applies to shows as
  it does to the chat.
- **If someone pays you to play something, say so on the air.** AuralFM sells no
  placement, and a show cannot pay its way up the globe or a list.
- **AuralFM does not record, keep or carry your show.** The sound goes from your
  device to your listeners.
- **AuralFM can stop listing a show.** For a report, a breach of these terms or
  a valid copyright notice (see Copyright, above), we take the host off the
  globe and out of the lists in our apps, and our apps stop playing the show.
  In appropriate circumstances we stop listing hosts who infringe repeatedly.
  We list a show again after a valid counter-notice. Stopping a listing does not
  reach the host's own device: what is sent from it is the host's to answer for.

The app asks you to confirm that you are 18 or over and that what you play is
yours to play **every time** before you go live or announce a show, and will not
put a show on the air without both.
