# AuralFM — Code of Conduct

**Last updated: 26 September 2026**

AuralFM is a way to hear each other's radio, from every corner of the world, with
nobody ranking who gets heard. That only works if the people in it can be there
without being hounded out. These rules apply wherever people speak on AuralFM:
the chat today, and station profiles, tips and shows as they arrive.

## Not here

- **Harassment.** Threats, pile-ons, following someone from room to room,
  sexual remarks at someone who did not ask for them.
- **Hate.** Attacking people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, caste,
  religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.
- **Anything that sexualises a child.** No exceptions, no context that excuses
  it. It is reported to the authorities (below).
- **Other people's private lives.** Their address, phone number, workplace or
  intimate images, shared without their consent.
- **Calls to violence**, and content that promotes terrorism or self-harm.
- **Scams.** Fake giveaways, phishing links, and above all **a payment or
  Lightning address that is not yours** — posing as a station or a receiver's
  owner to collect tips meant for them.
- **Impersonation** of a station, a person, or AuralFM.
- **Spam.** Flooding a room, advertising, bots passing as people.
- **Other people's work.** Rebroadcasting a stream, or sharing recordings, you
  have no right to.

## Stations

A listing is not an endorsement. AuralFM lists stations whatever their politics
or faith, because hearing a place as it sounds to itself is the point, and we do
not take a station off the globe for its opinions.

We do take a station off for broadcasting calls to violence against people,
material that sexualises children, or anything else plainly illegal; for being a
scam dressed as a station; or because the station asks us to.

## What you can do

- **Mute anyone.** Click their name in the chat. The mute is kept on your own
  device, and you can undo it under Settings.
- **Filter words.** Settings keeps a word list; messages containing them are
  hidden from you.
- **Report it.** Email **hello@auralfm.xyz** with the place you were listening
  to, roughly when, and what was said. A screenshot helps.
- **Material that sexualises a child:** do not copy or forward it, even to
  report it. Report it directly to NCMEC's CyberTipline at
  [report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org), or to the police where you
  are, and then tell us.

## What we can do, honestly

The chat runs over public Nostr relays run by other people. **Nobody at AuralFM
can delete a message from them**, and we will not pretend otherwise. What we can
do today: ask the relay operators to remove it, report it to the authorities
where the law requires, and take a station off the catalogue.

Next on the roadmap is a shared block list the app applies for everyone, so one
report protects every listener rather than only the one who muted.

As AuralFM adds things it hosts itself — station profiles, and later shows people
broadcast — enforcement grows with them: removal, suspension, and reports to the
authorities wherever the law requires them.

## Changes

If these rules change, the date at the top changes with them.
