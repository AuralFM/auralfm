MONEY
No paid tier, ever. A paid tier requires knowing who paid, and knowing who paid
is the one thing the app cannot do. This is architectural, not a pricing
preference.
No advertising, in the app or on the website. Not negotiable.
No App Store. Direct download, notarised, so nothing is gated by review.
Polar.sh rejected 5 Aug: flat-fee shape is wrong for a $5 tier.
GitHub Sponsors first. Open Collective later, once there is a real expense to
show. An empty ledger with a 10% host fee is overhead pretending to be
transparency.

ARCHITECTURE
Nostr over AT Protocol, for one narrow reason: Nostr needs no account at all.
The key is made when the app opens and thrown away when it closes. AT Protocol
assumes a persistent identity, which is the thing this app refuses to hold.
MeshLLM refused. Pooling community compute for in-app features contradicts the
no-backend position.
Captions built, measured at 39.6s for 6s of audio on the target hardware, and
switched off. Not promised.

RECEIVERS
Nine commitments, seven enforced in the app's code and two in the crawler.
Operators can opt out with one email and are dropped from the next build.
Three receivers were taken offline by over-probing during development. This is
why the rules exist and why it gets said out loud rather than buried.

WRITING
Theory, essays and reading notes live in Buzz and the vault, not in the GitHub
repo. Decided 6 Aug: a codebase is not where thinking belongs.
The repo stays private until code signing and a release exist.
