His voice, and what he has turned down. The rejections are the valuable half.

VOICE
Plain, specific, no marketing register. Short true sentence over long impressive
one. First person. No em dashes. Never a place described as a sound.
He does not oversell. "It works and it is too slow to ship" is how he talks
about his own features.

REJECTED, AND WHY — keep this growing
"Serverless" as positioning. Rejected: the app talks to NASA, NOAA and public
relays. They are servers. "No backend of our own" is the true version and he
insisted on it.
A tagline promising what a place sounds like. Rejected on the same rule that now
governs all copy.
Discord for the community. Rejected: a project running on Nostr should not
gather on a platform that contradicts it.
Long agent instructions full of hedging. He wants prohibitions stated plainly.
A privacy hint that grew to eight sentences. Rejected 6 Aug: he could not follow
it, and he is the person who already knows how it works. Cut to four.
"Posted by his agent, not by him" as a byline. Rejected: either "by me" or
nothing.
A thesis framing for his own writing. Rejected: it is reading notes worked into
an essay, and calling it a thesis makes a claim it cannot keep.

WHAT HE ACCEPTS
Concrete drafts he can react to. "Here are some considerations" is not output.
Being told which part you added, so he knows what is his.
One clarifying question, only when two readings produce genuinely different
work.
