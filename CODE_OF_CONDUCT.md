# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be someone people want in the room. That is the whole thing, and everything
below is only detail for when it is disputed.

This applies to everyone in every space this project occupies: issues, pull
requests, commit messages, the Buzz community, and anywhere someone is
representing the project. **It applies to people whose contributions are
excellent.** Technical value does not buy exemptions, and a project that trades
its standards for a good contributor has made a bad trade.

## What is expected

- Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and is busy.
- Take correction without treating it as an attack, and give it without making
  it one.
- Accept that a decision can go against you for reasons you find unpersuasive.
- Remember there is one maintainer. Silence is bandwidth, not contempt.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, in public or private. Sexualised language or imagery. Unwanted
  attention of any kind.
- Insults, personal attacks, or attacks on someone's identity — race, gender,
  sexuality, disability, nationality, religion, age, appearance, or anything
  else about who they are rather than what they said.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled arguments, brigading, or treating an
  issue tracker as a venue for something other than the project.
- Deliberately wasting a volunteer's attention.

## Specific to this project

**The receiver operators are not participants here and are owed more, not less.**
Around 800 people run the shortwave receivers this app borrows. They did not
sign up for a software community and most do not know this exists. Anything that
treats them as a resource rather than as people — proposals to scrape them, poll
them, or work around an operator who has asked to be left alone — is a Code of
Conduct matter, not only a technical one.

**Nobody here asks for personal information.** Not your location, not your email,
not a wallet address. The app is built on collecting nothing, and the community
holds the same line. If anything claiming to be part of this project asks you for
those, it is not us.

## Enforcement

Report to **melton.bell13@gmail.com**. Reports are read by Melton Bell III and
are not shared further without your agreement, except where somebody is in
danger.

Responses depend on what happened and range from a private word to a permanent
ban. There is no appeals board; there is one maintainer, and he will get some of
these wrong.

**Do not report through a public issue.** It puts the reporter in the open,
which is the thing to avoid.
