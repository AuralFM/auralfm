# Asset attribution

## Globe icons

Globe by **emilegraphics** via [the Noun Project](https://thenounproject.com).

| File | Source | Bands | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `icon.svg` | `noun-globe-1654366.svg` | filled | Menu bar, favicon, and the .icns entries ≤64px |
| `icon-line.svg` | `noun-globe-1654367.svg` | outlined | The .icns entries ≥128px, About panel |

**Two glyphs, on purpose.** The outlined globe is beautiful at 128 and above and
collapses into a solid disc at 32, because its latitude lines merge once they are
under a pixel apart. The filled globe keeps its bands legible down to 16.
Carrying different artwork at different sizes is what the `.icns` format is for.

Both adapted for AuralFM: cropped from a 100×125 viewBox to 100×100, embedded
attribution text removed, and `fill` set to `currentColor`.

**No separate light/dark files are needed.** `currentColor` makes each icon
inherit the surrounding text color, so one file renders correctly on both a
light and a dark menu bar.

**Licence: royalty-free, purchased.** Noun Project's free tier is CC BY —
unrestricted use as long as the creator is credited wherever the app is
distributed. The paid royalty-free licence removes that requirement, and it has
been bought for these icons.

The credit stays in the About panel anyway. It costs nothing, it is true, and
crediting the person who drew the thing is the right default whether or not a
licence compels it.

**Keep the receipt.** If AuralFM is ever distributed commercially, the purchase
record is the only proof the licence was obtained. Store it alongside this file
or in the project's records, not only in an email.
